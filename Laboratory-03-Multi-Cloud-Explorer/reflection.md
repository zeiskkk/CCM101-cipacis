# Mission Reflection

During this laboratory activity, I learned more about the differences and similarities between AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. The cloud platform that impressed me the most was AWS because it provides a wide range of services for different types of applications. I also learned that having many services does not automatically mean that a provider is the best choice because the needs of the organization must still be considered.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide computing, storage, networking, databases, identity management, and other cloud services. They also allow organizations to scale their resources without depending completely on physical infrastructure. However, each provider has different strengths. Azure has strong integration with Microsoft technologies, while Google Cloud is strong in artificial intelligence, data, and Kubernetes.

Business requirements have a major influence on cloud platform selection. For example, a company that already uses Microsoft 365 and Windows Server may benefit more from Azure. On the other hand, an AI research company may prefer Google Cloud because of its AI and data services. A startup may choose AWS because of its wide range of services and scalability.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that choosing a cloud provider requires more than simply choosing the most popular platform. I need to consider the client's budget, existing technology, application requirements, scalability, and future plans before making a recommendation.
